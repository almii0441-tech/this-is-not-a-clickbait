RECOVERED TEXT / POEM 006

It is quite a shock
When, in time’s embrace,
One’s creations are locked
From the viewer’s gaze.

And why is it so? All because
The superficial likes are not enough
There is a lack, or deficit, of
A single values in digital troughs.

I myself have experienced this
When, in hopes of momentary bliss,
I posted an pic of my own life,
And found myself faced by artificial strife.

I call these “Ghosts of the Feed”,
For they are invisible, indeed,
Yet they leave their targets in dread,
Fearing their works as if they’re unfed.
